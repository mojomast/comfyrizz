Static mark

This proof sheet has no animation, autoplay, transitions, glow or drop shadows. When prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is set, the page remains fully static and the two Z forms hold their final aligned position. Motion is optional and never required to read the identity.

Proof points

  • Lowercase wordmark reads comfyrizz with exactly two final z letters.
  • Symbol geometry is shared across the standalone mark, lockups and social card.
  • No embedded bitmaps, text elements, fonts, filters or gradients in the SVGs.
  • Paper and ink surfaces both use the token palette.
  • Small sizes use the hand-tuned favicon below 24 px.
  • Clear space and minimum widths follow the brand guide.
