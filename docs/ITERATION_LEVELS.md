# Iteration levels

Before a production spends anything on Google Colab, you choose how many versions to try. The choice is Draft, Balanced, Director, or Custom. Nothing is selected for you. Balanced is the highlighted recommendation when the production has no history.

Choosing a level sets how many takes, rounds, and auditions the production may ask for. It is not spend approval. Every paid plan still needs its own approval, and the number that binds that approval is the plan's reservation ceiling.

## The four levels

| Level | What you get |
|---|---|
| Draft | One version of everything. Fastest. Picked for you. |
| Balanced | Two voice options, two image options, and two versions of each shot. You pick the best version. |
| Director | Two scripts, three voice options, three image options, and up to six versions of each shot over two rounds. You pick at every stage. |
| Custom | The Advanced grid. Any edit to a preset becomes Custom. |

Easy mode shows the three presets and a Recommended card. Advanced mode shows the per-stage grid, the per-shot overrides, and a running total of video takes. A saved level can be changed later. Rendered shots and shots already in flight keep the level they started with.

## Estimates

Each level shows a low, expected, and high range of Google Colab units. The range is an estimate. Other sessions may be running on the same account, and an unmeasured GPU rate is labelled an unmeasured estimate. Money appears only when you have entered a price per unit.

With no spend cap, the estimate says "No spend cap set" and does not block the choice. Over the cap, the card says so and suggests a lower level that fits. The estimate never replaces the reservation ceiling on the plan.

## The gate

A paid plan cannot be approved until a level is stored on the production. Paid work is a render, a take, or an audition that can reserve Google Colab units. A local Kokoro audition does not. Free work, such as a brief or a script, can start before the choice.

The plan card hides Approve and shows the picker until the level exists. If the level changes after a plan was proposed, that plan is not rewritten. The card says "level changed — re-propose", and the next proposal follows the new level.

## Auto-pick

Auto-pick is allowed only for a stage you set to auto. The system may then select a landed take with actor `system` and reason `iteration:auto-pick`. That selection is not a spend approval and never resolves a final delivery or an action-spend decision. Ask, and hero shots, wait for you.

The agent can read the level and must stay inside it. It cannot set or raise the level. When you ask for more takes than the level allows, it asks you to change the level instead of proposing the extra work.
