# Next scene — “Save the Failure”

**Placement:** episode two, after the regulator race and return to the shelter.
**Status:** rewritten shooting draft; not generated.
**Target:** 60 seconds, 20 editorial shots. Rock-led repair action with cheeky
dialogue and a visible AI retry/memory payoff.

## What changes

Turbo starts with three engines fighting each other. By the end, the crew has
staggered their pulses and held one small shelter through an actual storm wave.
Hermes retains the failed method and the correction for the finale. Don helps
without being the fastest or winning the missing regulator.

The two rescued workers are warm and safe in the background zone. They do not
produce a secret part or have to earn their rescue. Molty and the regulator remain
away at the rival rig; do not insert them as workshop teammates.

## Screen geography and sound

Three engine housings run left to right along the rear wall. Turbo works at the
left control station; Don braces the right mounting beam. The shelter canopy is
visible through the open bay. Establish those positions before close inserts.

144 BPM original guitar/bass/drums; begin with a loose bolt hitting on a snare
accent. Palm-muted riff under repair, a one-beat stop before the test, full-band
return when the canopy visibly holds. Reduce guitars through every line. Keep
engine pulses distinct enough that the audience can hear simultaneous beats
become staggered beats. The music is accompaniment, not a magic control signal.

## Generation and edit notes

- Generate speaking shots with one visible speaker. Hermes can speak over a
  verified control insert; do not create an extra humanoid character for the voice.
- Author separate keys for beam slipping, brace locked and stable canopy. Every
  shot starts from the state the previous shot actually established.
- Render handles, then trim to measured speech and completed contact. Editorial
  timing is the target; do not accelerate voices to fit a failed take.
- Every joke names or reacts to the action already underway. The audience should
  follow the scene even without understanding retry loops or reusable skills.
- The final successful trace must match the staggered pattern that gets retrieved
  in episode five. Keep the failure as well: that is the causal payoff.
