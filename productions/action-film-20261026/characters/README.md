# Character bible stubs

Three placeholders for new non-human characters. They are fill-in templates, not
final art. Names, materials and expressions below are working prompts to be replaced
during setup/boards. Requirements from the research: distinct silhouettes, tactile
materials, **simple faces**, and different movement rhythms. Plates establish
full-body proportions and useful angles; they are not scene compositions.

| File | Handle | Silhouette | Movement rhythm |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`char-a-heavy.md`](char-a-heavy.md) | `char_a_heavy` | Low, wide, squared | Slow, weighty, stop-and-go |
| [`char-b-spindly.md`](char-b-spindly.md) | `char_b_spindly` | Tall, narrow, angular | Quick, staccato, precise |
| [`char-c-small.md`](char-c-small.md) | `char_c_small` | Small, round, compact | Springy, continuous, bouncy |

Check silhouettes against each other at thumbnail size: if two read alike, redesign.
Do not give a character a speaking voice unless the script needs one; any new voice
follows the [voice casting standard](../../../docs/VOICE_GENERATION.md).
