How to listen

Make the choice with your ears.

  1. Listen once without reading the delivery instructions.
  2. Use blind A/B to shuffle the order and hide the configuration labels.
  3. Check the alternate Qwen take. A useful setting should work beyond one lucky sample.
  4. Prefer delivery that fits the meaning. More emotion, slower speech or a different voice is not automatically better.

The pair downloads always play before → 0.8-second gap → candidate after. They are not shuffled by blind mode. Raw originals and exact settings are included in the download.
